# SearchBloom Competitive Research — What Ascent Can Steal, Adapt, and Beat

Deep-dive research on [searchbloom.com](https://www.searchbloom.com/), an award-winning
mid-market SEO agency, conducted to extract ideas for Ascent (autonomous SEO agent SaaS).

**Method note:** searchbloom.com actively blocks automated readers (HTTP 403), so this
research was assembled from search-engine indexed snippets of their own pages plus
third-party sources (Clutch, G2, DesignRush, Glassdoor, press releases, interviews).
Facts are tagged with confidence where it matters. Sources cited inline.

---

- Positioning: **"Partners, Not Clients"** / "Results You Can Count On" / data-driven search marketing for **mid-market** (Forbes Advisor named them best SEO agency for mid-market). ([homepage](https://www.searchbloom.com/), [/company/](https://www.searchbloom.com/company/))
- Self-reported flagship stats repeated site-wide: **98% partner retention**, **720%+ average campaign ROI**, 2.1% team turnover, $100M+ ad spend managed. (homepage, [best-SEO listicle](https://www.searchbloom.com/strategy/best-seo-companies-services/), [/ai-information/](https://www.searchbloom.com/ai-information/))
- Google Premier Partner (top 3%); awards from Backlinko, Forbes Advisor, USA Today, Search Engine Land, Clutch, DesignRush; 3x Inc. Best Place to Work. ([/awards/](https://www.searchbloom.com/awards/))

## 2. Their methodologies (the crown jewels)

They brand **five proprietary methodologies**, one per discipline ([/methodologies/](https://www.searchbloom.com/methodologies/)):

### A.R.T. — SEO (flagship)
- **Authority** — quality backlinks, digital PR, internal linking; E-E-A-T aligned; "links as votes."
- **Relevance** — keyword mapping, topic clusters, on-page optimization matched to search intent.
- **Technology** — site architecture, page speed, structured data, HTTPS, controlled indexation; "the foundation, not an afterthought."

### MERIT — AI Search Optimization (AEO/GEO), published as a whitepaper Oct 2025
5 pillars, 15 chapters ([whitepaper](https://www.searchbloom.com/merit-framework-whitepaper/), [press release](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/272708/)):
- **M — Mentions**: third-party validation (review platforms like G2/Clutch/Capterra "pay-to-play" placements, Reddit/Quora community sentiment, earned media).
- **E — Evidence**: original citable assets — original data, "information gain architecture," citation reinforcement.
- **R — Relevance**: answer-first content architecture, multi-format coverage (video is a primary AI citation surface — YouTube appears in 29.5% of AI Overviews per BrightEdge), semantic HTML + entity-rich language.
- **I — Inclusion**: entity optimization (Wikidata, Knowledge Panel), AI crawler access (robots.txt policy), IndexNow. (They consider llms.txt premature.)
- **T — Transformation**: citation-share measurement on 30/90-day moving averages, narrative/reputation alignment, org roles.

### Others
- **ACE** (PPC): Assets, Control, Experimentation.
- **UTU** (CRO): User experience, Trust signals, Urgency.
- **T.R.U.S.T.** (Web dev): Technical expertise, Reliable performance, User-first UX/UI, Secure frameworks, Transparent process.

### Supporting frameworks
- **Information Gain Score (IGS)**: `IGS = 1 − max cosine similarity` between your content's embedding and each top-ranking competitor's embedding; **≈0.50 is the citation threshold**. Plus a "5-to-7 Rule": competitive topics need 5–7 distinct original insights. ([information-gain-score](https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/information-gain-score/))
- **R-E-A-C-H content quality**: Real, Experienced, Accurate, Clear, Helpful. ([content frameworks guide](https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/seo-content-frameworks-guide/))
- Content process: topic identification → keyword **business-potential scoring** → ranking-difficulty assessment (backlink profiles of top pages) → search-intent alignment (match content **type, format, angle** to the SERP) → REACH quality bar → scheduled refreshes.
- **Query/intent taxonomy**: informational (long-tail, faster to rank), navigational, transactional, commercial. ([query types post](https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/understanding-query-types-search-intent/))

## 3. Their process & client experience

- Onboarding = discovery baseline across SEO/PPC/CRO → **structured 90-Day Roadmap** ("NDR" internally) with priorities, sequencing, dependencies, success measures — **rebuilt every 90 days** to avoid "same plan, different month" drift.
- Cadence: monthly performance reports, **bi-weekly strategy meetings**, custom dashboards, weekly emails "each with a handwritten explanation" (Clutch reviewer), **direct analyst access** ("dedicated analyst, not an account manager").
- Local SEO playbook: "the A.R.T. of Local SEO" — GBP posts/category strategy, review systems, location-page frameworks, call tracking with CRM tie-in, local link acquisition, citation scanning + NAP consistency. ([/local-seo/](https://www.searchbloom.com/local-seo/))
- Link building: manual outreach, guest posts, broken-link building; digital PR via data campaigns + reactive trend-jacking. ([digital PR page](https://www.searchbloom.com/link-building/digital-pr/))

## 6. Positioning weapons worth copying

- **Named, trademark-feeling methodologies** — ART/MERIT turn generic activity into a branded system ("predictable, repeatable").
- **Comparison pages** (13+ found: vs SEOBrand, KlientBoost, Victorious, Directive, etc.) comparing on methodology, contract terms, reporting cadence, support model, third-party ratings — with honest "choose X if…" verdicts.
- **Self-ranking listicles** ("Best SEO Companies 2026") in a dedicated `/strategy/` hub, ranking themselves #1 with a disclosed scoring rubric.
- **`/ai-information/` page** — explicit instructions for ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini with "official" company facts. An LLM-SEO tactic.
- Contract flexibility as a wedge: 3–6 month initial term then month-to-month, vs competitors' 12-month lock-ins.
- Every service page title: "Award-Winning…" + third-party validation ("Recognized by Backlinko").

## 7. Pricing (no public pricing page)

- Their blog: professional retainers **$2,500–$20,000/mo**, enterprise $100k+/mo. Their own floor: **SEO from ~$3,000/mo, local SEO ~$1,500/mo, PPC from $2,000/mo**. ([cost blog](https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-an-seo-company/))
- Clutch: min project $1,000+, $100–149/hr.
- No packages — "custom strategies" only → every deal requires a sales call. **Entry cost ≈ $18–36k/year.**

## 8. Weaknesses & gaps (Ascent's opening)

Their review quality is excellent (4.9/5 Clutch ~100 reviews) — attack the **model**, not the quality:

1. **Speed**: G2 reviewers cite slow development/integration times (landing pages, Google Ads, CRM hookups) and wish for "a more proactive approach to website management." Agencies check periodically; software monitors continuously.
2. **Key-person risk**: G2 reviewers fear losing their dedicated analyst; Glassdoor cites high turnover (publicly acknowledged by the CEO). Software doesn't quit.
3. **Reporting depth**: Clutch synthesis notes clients want "more actionable insights and detailed data analysis."
4. **Price excludes small business**: their own floor ($1.5–3k/mo) prices out the SMBs Ascent serves.
5. **Opacity**: no public pricing, no free trial, no self-serve product, no instant audit — everything gated behind a sales call. Industry data: **25% of agency firings are due to secrecy/lack of transparency; 29% due to missed long-term targets** ([Artios study](https://artios.io/why-seo-agencies-really-get-fired/)).
6. **Intelligence lock-in**: "the intelligence lived in the agency's tools, not yours" — switching costs drive renewals, not results.
7. **Human cadence**: 90-day roadmaps, bi-weekly meetings, monthly reports vs. an agent that cycles daily.

# Recommendations: what to implement in Ascent

## A. Agent behavior (the actual SEO engine)
1. **Adopt A.R.T. as the agent's internal audit rubric** — score every generated page on Authority (internal links in/out, linkable-asset quality), Relevance (intent match, topic-cluster fit), Technology (schema, speed hints, meta, heading structure). Surface the three sub-scores in the audit grade the dashboard already shows.
2. **Intent classification on every keyword** — tag keywords informational/commercial/transactional/local (we already show "Intent" in the Keywords tab; align to their taxonomy and have the agent prioritize long-tail informational first, since they rank faster).
3. **Business-potential scoring** — rank the content queue by conversion likelihood, not just volume (their content framework's key step).
4. **Information Gain check** — before publishing, compare draft embedding against top-ranking competitor pages; require a differentiation threshold. This is a genuinely defensible, technical feature (they publish the formula; we can actually automate it).
5. **REACH quality gate** — bake Real/Experienced/Accurate/Clear/Helpful into the generation prompt + audit.
6. **Schema on every page automatically** (they sell a manual generator; our agent should just do it — LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ).
7. **MERIT-lite AI-visibility features**: answer-first page architecture, entity-rich language, IndexNow pings on publish, AI-crawler-friendly robots directives, and later: AI citation tracking (does ChatGPT/AI Overviews cite the client?). This is the future-proof differentiator.
8. **90-day roadmap, automated**: the agent generates a rolling 90-day content roadmap and **rebuilds it from performance data every cycle** — their premium deliverable, produced continuously for free.

## B. Dashboard
1. **Overview**: add A.R.T.-style pillar scores; show "Roadmap" (next 90 days of planned pages) — the agent's version of their NDR.
2. **Keywords tab**: add business-potential score and intent-based prioritization; later, real volume/difficulty.
3. **Competitors tab**: add "keyword gap" framing (pages they rank for that you don't → each gap becomes a queued page); referring-domain counts as the authority metric they headline.
4. **Content tab**: show per-page audit grade with pillar breakdown; publish log with "committed & verified live" states (already in our console mockup).
5. **New later**: "AI Visibility" panel (citations in AI answers) — nobody at SMB price points offers this.

## C. Landing page
1. **Name our methodology.** The agent already runs research → plan → generate → enrich → score → publish; brand this loop (e.g. "the Ascent Cycle") and give it a section like their /methodologies/ page.
2. **Dollarized proof format**: when we have results, present them their way — headline metric in the title, Challenge → What the agent did → Results, and translate to revenue (sessions × AOV math).
3. **Agency-vs-Ascent comparison section/page**: cost ($3k+/mo vs our price), speed (weeks of onboarding vs minutes), cadence (monthly reports vs daily cycles), transparency (sales call vs public pricing + free trial), key-person risk (none). This attacks the 25%-secrecy / 29%-missed-targets firing data directly.
4. **Free instant tool as lead magnet**: an instant SEO audit / "keyword gap finder" (enter your domain + a competitor → see the gap) — their tools strategy, but self-serve and tied straight into our onboarding.
5. **/ai-information page + llms-friendly facts** for Ascent itself — cheap, clever GEO.

## D. Onboarding wizard
1. Add **AOV / average job value** field (Market step) so the dashboard can dollarize projections like their CRO studies.
2. Add optional **GBP connection** step alongside Search Console (their local playbook is GBP-heavy).
3. Keep collecting competitors, but frame it as "we'll map their keyword coverage against yours" (gap analysis promise).

## E. Pricing/packaging
1. **Public pricing, self-serve, free trial** — the exact three absences in their model. Anchor against agency cost: "An agency charges $3,000/month. Ascent starts at $X."
2. Position tiers by publish cadence (their $ scales with scope; ours scales with pages/cycles) — e.g. weekly / every-3-days / daily mapping to our existing cadence options.
3. Month-to-month always — they already proved contract flexibility sells against 12-month lock-ins; go further.
